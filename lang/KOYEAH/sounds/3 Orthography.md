## 3.1 Standardised Tawish orthography
* The Standardised Modern Tawish (post-Tawaistãnq standardisation but pre-Tawaistãnq register split) orthography is used
* This means each word is written as it is pronounced in Ashi Tawa, even though it isn't pronounced that way in Koyeah Tawa
* In the new orthography, note these changes:
	* *rh* for *rr*
