* Vowels are split into these groups:
	* Regular: *a i u e o*
	* Peripheral: *á í ú é ó*
	* Neutral vowels are not marked and are non-distinctive

## 1.3 Allophones
| Phoneme | Allophone | Condition                 |
| ------- | --------- | ------------------------- |
| nʲ      | ɲ         | /ɲ/ in consonant clusters |
| n       | ŋ         | /ŋ/ before velars         |
| h       | ç         | /ç/ before [i ɪ]          |
| h       | ɸ         | [ɸ] before [u]            |
* *n* [n] changes into *m* [m] before bilabials (e.g., *hon* + *ppu* -> *homppu*)
