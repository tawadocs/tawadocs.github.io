## 8.1 Relationals
* They aren't restricted to verbs:
	* *Ze byo i* :: `ITER DEM.PROX COP`
		* "(I am) back"
		* lit. "Again here (is / am)"
* The copular is a generic *to be* stative:
	* *Subute ni wan kita mo i* :: `desire.V-INF TOP one like COM COP`
		* "I want to be one with love"
		* "I want to be joined together with love"
