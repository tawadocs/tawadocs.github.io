* Make any pronoun a genitive by appending **-n**:
	* *san* -> hers (**asasan** - `house-3SG.F.POSS`, her house)
* Possession markers take a *t/d* form if the word ends in the same sequence:
	* *Dasun* + *-sun* -> *dasuntsun*
	* *Dazun* + *-zun* -> *dasundzun*
* Pronunciation is self-explanatory
* They can be dropped, since much of **Kakatte Koyeah** is a register of **Modern Tawa**, which is a register of **Southern Tawa**
* Use *a* (dummy pronoun) to refer to a subject:
	* *sa ni a kita* -> `3SG TOP A like`
		* "(As for) her, I like (her)"
	* if you did not use *a*:
		* *sa ni kita* -> `3SG TOP like`
			* "(As for her), she likes..."
