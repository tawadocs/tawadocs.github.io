## 5.1 Reduplication
* Koyeah Tawa uses either *monoreduplication* or *direduplication*
* Direduplication is less formal than monoreduplication, which is the only accepted form
* Trireduplication is considered taboo in many contexts:
	* *kita* -> like
	* *kikita* -> love
	* *kikikita* -> the maximal, god-like expression of love
		* in religious contexts, this term is strictly reserved for God Himself, not anything nor anyone else
