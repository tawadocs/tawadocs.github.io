## 4.1 Core adposition system
| Case    | Core usage                                                                                                                  |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ji**  | indirect object; location of existence; destination or final arrival point; time; goal of a movement; passive or causative  |
| **un**  | instrument or means; location of action; material or composition; cause or reason; limit                                    |
| **do**  | traversal or route of motion; point of detachment                                                                           |
| **da**  | heading (towards); progression                                                                                              |
| **dan** | temporospatial starting point or origin; source of material; source of information / giving                                 |
| **mo**  | comitative; quotation or boundary of thought or sound; comparison; temporal (and spatial) sequentiality; contrasting linker |
| **we**  | additive (and)                                                                                                              |
- In Koyeah Tawa, many cases have turned into postpositions
- They function virtually the same:
    - **do**
        - traversal or route of _motion_
            - flew (through) the _sky_
                - _kiuku sora do_
            - walked (along) the _path_
                - _haeku tachi do_
        - point of detachment
            - leave (from) _home_
                - _kuenau asa do_
            - get (out of) the _cart_
                - _ea eanu-natse do_
    - **ji**
        - indirect object
            - give a gift **to** a friend
                - _koha kohaoe onokoi ji_ -> `gift gift-NM friend DAT`
        - location of existence
            - food is **on** the table
                - _kamo jirei ji_ -> `food.NM TOP table LOC`
        - destination or final arrival point
            - we are **at** her home
                - _na asasan ji_ -> `3SG house-3SG.F.POSS LOC`
            - we flew **to** London
                - _na kiuku Landan ji_ -> `3SG fly.V london LOC`
        - time
            - writing words **at** 2:55 AM
                - _payaen kana 0255 AM ji_ -> `write-NMLZ word 0328 am LOC`
        - goal of a movement
            - went **out to** shop
                - _ea hari ji_ -> `MOV buy DAT`
        - passive or causative
            - the boss made the employee write the report
                - _komuni komuninq ji a monu tetoeka_
                - "the boss **made** the employee write the report"
    - **un**
        - instrument or means (DON'T confuse with comitative 'mo')
            - wrote the book **digitally** / **in/via** electronic interface
                - _paya ribuoe tahete un_ -> `write book-NM electronic INSTR`
            - ate **with** a spoon
                - _kamu oho un_ -> `eat-V spoon INSTR`
        - location of action
            - had fun **at** her house
                - _hahaen asasan un_ -> `laugh-NMLZ house-3SG.F.POSS INSTR`
        - material or composition
            - made **of / by / with** wood
                - _ru tsu un_ -> `CAUS.V wood INSTR`
        - cause or reason
            - he did not come **as** he was sick
                - _so yarara mahwa un_ -> `3SG.M come-NEG sick CAUSAL`
        - limit (time/quantity/scope)
            - the tool is cheap **for** its high quality
                - _ryo twana ni lili mani i poki iioe un_ -> `tool TOP little money COP big good-NM LMT`
                    - lit. "as for the tool, it is of little money for its big goodness"
            - finished work **in** 3 days
                - _kiwinq irime 3 ko un_ -> `continue-INV work three day PER`
    - **da**
        - heading (towards)
            - heading **towards** the mountains
                - _ea asasan da_ -> `MOV house-3SG.F.POSS ALL`
                    - p.s., here, the focus is on 'active direction,' not the destination, unlike _ji_
        - progression
            - going **to / progressing** towards London
                - _ea Landan da_ -> `MOV london ALL`
    - **dan**
        - spatial starting point
            - I came here **from** Canada
                - _yaranai Canada dan_ -> `come-MID canada ABL`
        - temporal origin
            - I started **from** almost 1 to 2 o'clock
                - _kiwi 1 ato 2 Pi'Em dambu_ -> `continue one or two pm ABL-PROP`
                    - *dambu* is an approximate point due to presence of **-bu**
                    - because the following consonant is bilabial, *dan* changes to *dam*
        - source of material
            - drug **from** fruits
                - _kara ba dan_
                    - p.s., here, it is about transformation or change itself; in _un_, the original material was wood, but with *dan*, the original material has changed (fruits to drugs, seed to plant, etc.)
        - source of information / giving
            - heard about it **from** her
                - _fofunai ryo ita sa dan_ -> `speak.V-IV DEM.PROX PRTV 3SG.F ABL`
    - **mo**
        - comitative
            - I never argue **with** my sister
                - _shahadara pashachasun mo_ -> `fight-NEG sibling-F-1SG.POSS COM`
        - quotation or boundary of a thought or sound
            - the bell rang **with** a ding
                - _kare iyuku mo 'jing'_ -> `bell sound.V QUOT ding`
            - he said **that** "I will come"
                - _so fofu mo yaranai_ -> `3SG speak.V QUOT come-MID`
        - comparison (glossed as `CMPR`)
            - bigger **than** him
                - _iepoki mo so_ -> `AUG-big COMP 3SG.M`
        - temporal sequence or sequentiality
            - ate **then** went to talk to him
                - _kamu mo eate fofu ji so_ -> `eat.V SEQ go-INF speak.V DAT 3SG.M`
        - contrasting linker (as **mo mo**)
            - **but** I'm eating
                - _mo mo su kamen_ -> `but 1SG eat.NMLZ`
            - you can do it **but** only if you pay first
                - _nasute fu, mo mo wan ji, mani_ -> `ability.V-INF ACT but first LOC money`
    - **ita**
        - aboutness, regarding
            - speak **about** her
                - _fofu sa ita_ -> `speak.V 3SG.F PRTV`
        - circumambulate
            - walk **about / around** a house
                - _haekukunh asa ita_ -> `walk.V-LIT house PRTV`
                - p.s. use _haekukunh_ if _haeku_ alone is ambiguous, as _haeku_ can mean to learn as well (e.g., learn about a house)
        - partitive
            - drink **some of** this water
                - _kamu chi no ita_ -> `eat.V water GEN PRTV`
            - **some of** the people like me
                - _hon no ita kita su_ -> `person GEN PRTV like 1SG`
            - **not yet done** sending the picture
                - _senoden no ita emonoe_ -> `send.NMLZ GEN PRTV picture.NM`
    - **itan**
        - avoiding, focusing (away) from the centre
            - why are you **beating around the bush regarding** her?
                - _zu fofu sa itan_ -> `2SG speak.V 3SG.F EX`
        - exclude
            - walk **avoiding** the house
                - _haeku asa itan_ -> `walk.V house EX`
        - totalitive
            - drink **all of** this water
                - _kamu chi no itan_ -> `eat.V water GEN TOT`
            - **all of** the people like me
                - _hon no itan kita su_ -> `person GEN TOT like 1SG`
            - **completed / fully done** sending the picture:
                - _senoden no itan emonoe_ -> `send.NMLZ GEN TOT picture.NM`
    - **po**
        - being in favour or supporting
            - she is for him / for his favour / in favour of him
                - _sa so po i_ -> `3SG.F FOR 3SG.M COP`
        - positioned beside or alongside a thing
            - I am beside the house **(poji)**
                - _su asa asapoji i_ -> `1SG house FOR-LOC COP`
    - **bo**
        - being against
            - she is against him
                - _sa so bo i_ -> `3SG.F 3SG.M CNTR COP`
        - resting against or using a thing as support **(boji)**
            - I am opposite to / against the house
                - _su asa boji i_ -> `1SG house CNTR-LOC COP`
            - I'm resting against the house's walls
                - _su a rawa asa no ga boji_ -> `1SG A rest side GEN house CNTR-LOC`
        - acting as substitute (instead of)
            - we need peace instead of war
                - _na a anisu ritaruoe shahada boji_ -> `1PL A require.V soft-NM war CNTR-LOC`
    - **we**
        - additive
            - apples **and** bananas (and as such)
                - _atora we karaba_ -> `apple ADD creamfruit`

## 4.3 Uses
* They're essentially used in the same way as described in:
	* **Ashi Tawa, Grammar:17-18**
* You always use the *core* preposition particles and **ONLY IF** nuance is needed, you use the other available ones
* More often than not, particles like *ye* or *yen* are not needed
* The only exception to this is *ita* and maybe some of the locative class

## 4.4 The 'wa' word particle
* Apart from its domains as per Ashi Tawa grammar, it also inherits a domain from the Japanese も (**mo**) particle, like in:
	* *ii hon oi wa futa a unsunai* :: `good person EXCESS-COP too cap A live.V-MID`
		* "There has to be a limit to being too much of a good person!"
			* literally, *wa* here is like saying 'there also/even has to be a limit...' / 'there has to be a limit as well / too'
			* however, more properly, it's behaving akin to a comitative
* The difference between *mo* and *wa*:
	* *wa* is an inclusive and additive
		* *su wa ii* :: ~"I am also good / I am good too"
	* *mo* is a relational and sequential and has many functions
		* *su ii mo* :: ~"Me with good"
		* *su mo ii* :: ~"Me, then good"
* Keep this in mind when choosing which to use:
	* *mo*: here is how A relates to B
		* *ii hon oi futa a unsunai mo*
			* being too good comes with a limit that exists
		* *ii hon oi mo futa a unsunai*
			* being too good, then a limit exists
	* *wa*: here is one more thing to consider
		* *ii hon oi wa futa a unsunai*
			* there has to be a limit to being too good...
* ... and note that:
	* *wa* can replace *a* and *ni* naturally, and it can also topicalise clauses
	* *mo* is a postposition and can't do this - and because the language highly prefers SVO (unless it's formal or complex/technical writing), using *mo* actually makes the phrase harder to parse (unless reordered in SOV, which is redundant already) and more redundant
