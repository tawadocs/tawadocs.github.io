## 3.1 Particle table
| Particle | IPA     | Gloss |
| -------- | ------- | ----- |
| **me**   | `/me/`  | REL   |
| **mas**  | `/mas/` | AGNR  |

## 3.2 Nesting
* Nesting is often just handled by **me**:
	* *hatero ngoshi a kamu mafo atora me unsu* :: `fast bird A eat.V red apple REL live.V`
		* lit. "Fast bird eats red apple - lives"
		* ~"The fast bird that ate the red apple lives"

## 3.2 Recursion
* Just embed a *me-clause* into a *me-clause*:
	* *ngoshi a kamu atora me nandanai ika tsu me unsu* :: `bird A eat.V apple REL down-MID ABL tree REL live.V`
		* The bird that ate the apple that fell from the tree lives

## 3.3 Clausal description
* *me* is also used for this, but often paired with a copular:
	* *hon me korumas i* :: `person REL kill.V-AGNR COP`
		* "The person that is a killer"
	* *ii hon a kitate yume ribu me iinq i* :: `good person A like-INF read book REL good-ANT COP`
		* ~"The person that likes to read books is (actually) bad"
* Do note that the copular may be omitted, so:
	* *hon me korumas*
* ... is fine
