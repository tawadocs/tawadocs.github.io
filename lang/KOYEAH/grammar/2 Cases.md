## 2.1 Kakatte Koyeah case system
| Case      | Modern / simplified domain                                                                                                               |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **õ**    | direct object; traversal or route of motion; point of detachment                                                                         |
| **ji**    | indirect object; location of existence; destination or final arrival point; time; goal of a movement; passive or causative               |
| **un**    | instrument or means; location of action; material or composition; cause or reason; limit                                                 |
| **lla**   | heading (towards); progression                                                                                                           |
| **llanh** | temporospatial starting point or origin; source of material; source of information / giving                                              |
| **mo**    | comitative; additive; quotation or boundary of thought or sound;  comparison; temporal (and spatial) sequentiality; contrasting linker   |
| **ita**   | a piece or some of a thing; aboutness or an incomplete action                                                                            |
| **itanh** | the entirety of a thing; completion of an action; total embodiment of a thing                                                            |
| **po**    | positioned beside or alongside a thing; being in favour or supporting                                                                    |
| **bo**    | positioned opposite or facing something; resting against or using a thing as support; being against or acting as substitute (instead of) |

## 2.2 Explanation and definition
* In Koyeah Tawa, many cases have multiple functions:
	* **o**
		* direct object
		* often left unmarked
			* read the *book*
				* *ake paya ribuo*
				* *ake paya o ribuo*
					* ~"Oh, damn it, just read the book!"
		* traversal or route of *motion*
			* flew (through) the *sky*
				* *kiuku o sorao*
			* walked (along) the *path*
				* *haeku o tachio*
		* point of detachment
			* leave (from) *home*
				* *kuenau o asao*
			* step (off) the *bus*
	* **ji**
		* indirect object
			* give a gift **to** a friend
				* *koha o kohao ji onokoi* -> `gift ACC gift-NM DAT friend`
		* location of existence
			* food is **on** the table
				* *kamo ji jirei* -> `food.NM TOP LOC LOC-INANIM`
		* destination or final arrival point
			* we are **at** her home
				* *na ji asasan* -> `3SG LOC house-3SG.F.POSS`
			* we flew **to** London
				* *na kiuku ji Landan* -> `3SG fly.V LOC london`
		* time
			* writing words **at** 3:27 PM
				* *payaen kana ji 0328 PM* -> `write-NMLZ word LOC 0328 PM`
		* goal of a movement
			* went **out to** shop
				* *ea ji hari* -> `MOV DAT buy`
		* passive or causative
			* the boss made the employee write the report
				* *komuni komuninqji monu tetoka*
				* "the boss **made** the employee write the report"
	* **un**
		* instrument or means
			* wrote the book **digitally** / **in/via** electronic interface
				* *paya o ribuo un tahete* -> `write ACC book-NM INSTR electronic`
			* ate **with** a spoon
				* *kamu un oho* -> `eat-V INSTR spoon`
		* location of action
			* had fun **at** her house
				* *hahaen un asasan* -> `laugh-NMLZ INSTR house-3SG.F.POSS`
		* material or composition
			* made **of / by / with** wood
				* *ru un tsu* -> `CAUS.V INSTR wood`
		* cause or reason
			* he did not come **as** he was sick
				* *so yarara un mahwa* -> `3SG.M come-NEG CAUSAL sick`
		* limit (time/quantity/scope)
			* the tool is cheap **for** its high quality
				* *twana ni lili mani un poki iio* -> `tool TOP little money LMT big good-NM`
					* lit. "as for the tool, it is of little money for its big goodness"
			* finished work **in** 3 days
				* *kiwinq irime un 3 ko* -> `continue-INV work PER three day`
	* **lla**
		* heading (towards)
			* heading **towards** the mountains
				* *ea lla asasan* -> `MOV ALL house-3SG.F.POSS`
					* p.s., here, the focus is on 'active direction,' not the destination, unlike *ji*
		* progression
			* going **to / progressing** towards London
				* *ea lla Landan* -> `MOV ALL london`
	* **llanh**
		* spatial starting point
			* I came here **from** Canada
				* *yara llanh Canada* -> `come ABL canada`
		* temporal origin
			* I started **from** almost 1 to 2 o'clock
				* *kiwi llambu 1 ato 2 Pi'Em* -> `continue ABL-PROP one or two pm`
					* *llambu* is an approximate point due to presence of **-bu**
		* source of material
			* drug **from** fruits
				* *kara llanh ba*
					* p.s., here, it is about transformation or change itself; in *un*, the original material was wood, but with *llanh*, the original material has changed (fruits to drugs, seed to plant, etc.)
		* source of information / giving
			* heard about it **from** her
				* *fofunai ita ryo llanh sa* -> `speak.V-IV PRTV DEM.PROX ABL 3SG.F`
	* **mo**
		* comitative
			* I never argue **with** my sister
				* *shahadara mo pashachasun* -> `fight-NEG COM sibling-F-1SG.POSS`
		* as an additive
			* apples **and** bananas (and as such)
				* *atora mo karaba* -> `apple ADD creamfruit`
		* quotation or boundary of a thought or sound
			* the bell rang **with** a ding
				* *kare iyuku mo 'jing'* -> `bell sound.V COM ding`
			* he said **that** he will come
				* *so fofu mo yara* -> `3SG speak.V QUOT come`
		* comparison
			* bigger **than** him
				* *iepoki mo so* -> `AUG-big COMP 3SG.M`
		* temporal sequence or sequentiality
			* ate **then** went to talk to him
				* *kamu mo ea ji fofu ji so* -> `eat.V SEQ go DAT speak.V DAT 3SG.M`
		* contrasting linker (as **mo mo**)
			* **but** I'm eating
				* *mo mo su kamen* -> `but 1SG eat.NMLZ`
			* you can do it **but** only if you pay first
				* *nasute fu, mo mo mani shi wan* -> `ability.V-INF ACT CONT money COND first`
	* **ita**
		* aboutness, regarding
			* speak **about** her
				* *fofu ita sa* -> `speak.V PRTV 3SG.F`
		* circumambulate
			* walk **about / around** a house
				* *haekukun ita asa* -> `walk.V-LIT PRTV house`
				* p.s. use *haekukun* if *haeku* alone is ambiguous, as *haeku* can mean to learn as well (e.g., learn about a house)
		* partitive
			* drink **some of** this water
				* *kamu chi no ita* -> `eat.V water GEN PRTV`
			* **some of** the people like me
				* *hon no ita kita su* -> `person GEN PRTV like 1SG`
			* **not yet done** sending the picture
				* *senoden o no ita emono* -> `send.NMLZ ACC GEN PRTV picture`
	* **itanh**
		* avoiding, focusing (away) from the centre
			* why are you **beating around the bush regarding** her?
				* *zu fofu itanh sa* -> `2SG speak.V EX 3SG.F`
		* exclude
			* walk **avoiding** the house
				* *haeku itanh asa* -> `walk.V EX house`
		* totalitive
			* drink **all of** this water
				* *kamu chi no itanh* -> `eat.V water GEN TOT`
			* **all of** the people like me
				* *hon no itanh kita su* -> `person GEN TOT like 1SG`
			* **completed / fully done** sending the picture:
				* *senoden o no itanh emono* -> `send.NMLZ ACC GEN TOT picture`
	* **po**
		* being in favour or supporting 
			* she is for him / for his favour / in favour of him
				* *sa po so* -> `3SG.F FOR 3SG.M`
		* positioned beside or alongside a thing
			* I am beside the house **(poji)**
				* *su poji asa* -> `1SG FOR-LOC house`
	* **bo**
		* being against
			* she is against him
				* *sa bo so* -> `3SG.F CNTR 3SG.M`
		* resting against or using a thing as support **(boji)**
			* I am opposite to / against the house
				* *su boji asa* -> `1SG CNTR-LOC house`
			* I'm resting against the house's walls
				* *sua rawa boji ga no asa* -> `1SG-A rest CNTR-LOC side GEN house`
		* acting as substitute (instead of)
			* we need peace instead of war
				* *na′a anisu o ritarusumata boji shahada* -> `1PL-A need.V soft-essence CNTR-LOC war`

## 2.3 Ashi Tawa case nouns
* In Ashi Tawa, cases like *ji* combined with *tei* to form nouns such as *jitei* (place)
* In Koyeah Tawa, this is possible, as it borrows Ashi Tawa vocabulary, even if it doesn't have *-tei*
* Note that Koyeah Tawa-specific cases can't combine with *-tei* to derive nouns, because *-tei* doesn't even exist here
