## 11.1 The middle voice
* The middle voice *-nai* can be a very difficult concept to grok, especially when you see it appearing everywhere without understanding what it does:
	* *Earranai* :: ~"I did not go"
	* *Earra* :: ~"I did not move (something)"
	* *Shirenai no?* :: ~"Do you know?"
	* *Shire no?* :: ~"Did you learn?"
	* *Shirenaite kamu* :: ~"I know how to eat"
	* *Shirete kamu* :: ~"I have learnt (how) to eat"
* Throughout all these examples, one thing remains true:
	* *nai* demotes an argument from a verb
* So:
	* *shire* (know) by default means to 'do knowing' of something (e.g., learn)
	* *ea* (move) by default means to 'move' something or it marks movement
* With *-nai*:
	* *shirenai* is the knowledge possessed itself:
		* *ake shire payazun ji!*
			* lit. "Do knowing (of something) for your exam!"
			* ~"Prepare for your test!"
			* the object of *shire* is implied, as it is omitted
			* you can specify:
				* *Senodzu zu ji kiteioe ni, ake, shire paya ji!*
					* lit. "I sent to you a thing, as for it, know it for the exam!"
					* ~"Prepare from what I sent you for your exam!"
	* *eanai* is movement concerning the subject:
		* *to eanai asasan ji*
			* "I went to her home"
* Same rule for infinitives:
	* *shirete* -> to do knowing (infinitive)
		* *shirete au poki riwakango, fuka!*
			* lit. "Learn to have a big brain, please!"
				* but this is an inaccurate translation
				* *shirete*'s direct object is omitted, so it's like saying ~'you need to study to be smart'
	* *shirenaite* -> to know
		* *shirenaite au poki riwakango, fuka!*
			* lit. "Learn how to have a big brain, please!"
				* no direct object, so 'learn to have a big brain' is accurate here as well

## 11.2 Roles
* **Verb valency demotion (argument reduction)**
	* *hari* -> exchange X in relation to Y (e.g., for)
	* *harinai* -> buy X (with optional context; i.e., Y)
* **Marking intransitivity**
	* *shire* -> to do knowing (of) / to learn
		* *ake shire ryo!* -> learn this!
	* *shirenai* -> to know
		* *shirenai ryo ita no?* -> (do you) know about this?
* **Middle voice**
	* *sa a koru* -> she kills
	* *sa a korunai* -> she dies (and it is her fault) / she kills herself
	* *sa o korunai* -> she dies
* **Reciprocality (Agentive and patientive reflexive)**
	* *so a jitekunai* -> he pees himself (and it is his fault)
	* *so o jitekunai* -> he pees himself (accidentally)
