## 4.1 Core preposition system
| Case    | Core usage                                                                                                                  |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ji**  | indirect object; location of existence; destination or final arrival point; time; goal of a movement; passive or causative  |
| **un**  | instrument or means; location of action; material or composition; cause or reason; limit                                    |
| **do**  | traversal or route of motion; point of detachment                                                                           |
| **da**  | heading (towards); progression                                                                                              |
| **dan** | temporospatial starting point or origin; source of material; source of information / giving                                 |
| **mo**  | comitative; quotation or boundary of thought or sound; comparison; temporal (and spatial) sequentiality; contrasting linker |
| **we**  | additive (and)                                                                                                              |
- In Koyeah Tawa, many cases have multiple functions:
    - **do**
        - traversal or route of _motion_
            - flew (through) the _sky_
                - _kiuku do sora_
            - walked (along) the _path_
                - _haeku do tachi_
        - point of detachment
            - leave (from) _home_
                - _kuenau do asa_
            - get (out of) the _cart_
                - _ea do eanu-natse_
    - **ji**
        - indirect object
            - give a gift **to** a friend
                - _koha kohaoe ji onokoi_ -> `gift gift-NM DAT friend`
        - location of existence
            - food is **on** the table
                - _kamo ji jirei_ -> `food.NM TOP LOC table`
        - destination or final arrival point
            - we are **at** her home
                - _na ji asasan_ -> `3SG LOC house-3SG.F.POSS`
            - we flew **to** London
                - _na kiuku ji Landan_ -> `3SG fly.V LOC london`
        - time
            - writing words **at** 2:55 AM
                - _payaen kana ji 0255 AM_ -> `write-NMLZ word LOC 0328 am`
        - goal of a movement
            - went **out to** shop
                - _ea ji hari_ -> `MOV DAT buy`
        - passive or causative
            - the boss made the employee write the report
                - _komuni ji komuninq a monu tetoeka_
                - "the boss **made** the employee write the report"
    - **un**
        - instrument or means (DON'T confuse wth comitative 'mo')
            - wrote the book **digitally** / **in/via** electronic interface
                - _paya ribuoe un tahete_ -> `write book-NM INSTR electronic`
            - ate **with** a spoon
                - _kamu un oho_ -> `eat-V INSTR spoon`
        - location of action
            - had fun **at** her house
                - _hahaen un asasan_ -> `laugh-NMLZ INSTR house-3SG.F.POSS`
        - material or composition
            - made **of / by / with** wood
                - _ru un tsu_ -> `CAUS.V INSTR wood`
        - cause or reason
            - he did not come **as** he was sick
                - _so yarara un mahwa_ -> `3SG.M come-NEG CAUSAL sick`
        - limit (time/quantity/scope)
            - the tool is cheap **for** its high quality
                - _ryo twana ni lili mani i un poki iioe_ -> `tool TOP little money COP LMT big good-NM`
                    - lit. "as for the tool, it is of little money for its big goodness"
            - finished work **in** 3 days
                - _kiwinq irime un 3 ko_ -> `continue-INV work PER three day`
    - **da**
        - heading (towards)
            - heading **towards** the mountains
                - _ea da asasan_ -> `MOV ALL house-3SG.F.POSS`
                    - p.s., here, the focus is on 'active direction,' not the destination, unlike _ji_
        - progression
            - going **to / progressing** towards London
                - _ea da Landan_ -> `MOV ALL london`
    - **dan**
        - spatial starting point
            - I came here **from** Canada
                - _yaranai dan Canada_ -> `come-MID ABL canada`
        - temporal origin
            - I started **from** almost 1 to 2 o'clock
                - _kiwi dambu 1 ato 2 Pi'Em_ -> `continue ABL-PROP one or two pm`
                    - *dambu* is an approximate point due to presence of **-bu**
        - source of material
            - drug **from** fruits
                - _kara dam ba_
                    - p.s., here, it is about transformation or change itself; in _un_, the original material was wood, but with *dan*, the original material has changed (fruits to drugs, seed to plant, etc.)
                    - because the following consonant is bilabial, *dan* changes to *dam*
        - source of information / giving
            - heard about it **from** her
                - _fofunai ita ryo dan sa_ -> `speak.V-IV PRTV DEM.PROX ABL 3SG.F`
    - **mo**
        - comitative
            - I never argue **with** my sister
                - _shahadara mo pashachasun_ -> `fight-NEG COM sibling-F-1SG.POSS`
        - quotation or boundary of a thought or sound
            - the bell rang **with** a ding
                - _kare iyuku mo 'jing'_ -> `bell sound.V COM ding`
            - he said **that** he will come
                - _so fofu mo yaranai_ -> `3SG speak.V QUOT come-MID`
        - comparison
            - bigger **than** him
                - _iepoki mo so_ -> `AUG-big COMP 3SG.M`
        - temporal sequence or sequentiality
            - ate **then** went to talk to him
                - _kamu mo eate fofu ji so_ -> `eat.V SEQ go-INF speak.V DAT 3SG.M`
        - contrasting linker (as **mo mo**)
            - **but** I'm eating
                - _mo mo su kamen_ -> `but 1SG eat.NMLZ`
            - you can do it **but** only if you pay first
                - _nasute fu, mo mo ji wan, mani i_ -> `ability.V-INF ACT but LOC first money COP`
    - **ita**
        - aboutness, regarding
            - speak **about** her
                - _fofu ita sa_ -> `speak.V PRTV 3SG.F`
        - circumambulate
            - walk **about / around** a house
                - _haekukunh ita asa_ -> `walk.V-LIT PRTV house`
                - p.s. use _haekukunh_ if _haeku_ alone is ambiguous, as _haeku_ can mean to learn as well (e.g., learn about a house)
        - partitive
            - drink **some of** this water
                - _kamu chi no ita_ -> `eat.V water GEN PRTV`
            - **some of** the people like me
                - _hon no ita kita su_ -> `person GEN PRTV like 1SG`
            - **not yet done** sending the picture
                - _senoden no ita emonoe_ -> `send.NMLZ GEN PRTV picture.NM`
    - **itan**
        - avoiding, focusing (away) from the centre
            - why are you **beating around the bush regarding** her?
                - _zu fofu itan sa_ -> `2SG speak.V EX 3SG.F`
        - exclude
            - walk **avoiding** the house
                - _haeku itan asa_ -> `walk.V EX house`
        - totalitive
            - drink **all of** this water
                - _kamu chi no itan_ -> `eat.V water GEN TOT`
            - **all of** the people like me
                - _hon no itan kita su_ -> `person GEN TOT like 1SG`
            - **completed / fully done** sending the picture:
                - _senoden no itan emonoe_ -> `send.NMLZ GEN TOT picture.NM`
    - **po**
        - being in favour or supporting
            - she is for him / for his favour / in favour of him
                - _sa po so_ -> `3SG.F FOR 3SG.M`
        - positioned beside or alongside a thing
            - I am beside the house **(poji)**
                - _su poji asa_ -> `1SG FOR-LOC house`
    - **bo**
        - being against
            - she is against him
                - _sa bo so_ -> `3SG.F CNTR 3SG.M`
        - resting against or using a thing as support **(boji)**
            - I am opposite to / against the house
                - _su boji asa_ -> `1SG CNTR-LOC house`
            - I'm resting against the house's walls
                - _su a rawa boji ga no asa_ -> `1SG A rest CNTR-LOC side GEN house`
        - acting as substitute (instead of)
            - we need peace instead of war
                - _na a anisu ritaruoe boji shahada_ -> `1PL A require.V soft-NM CNTR-LOC war`
    - **we**
        - additive
            - apples **and** bananas (and as such)
                - _atora we karaba_ -> `apple ADD creamfruit`

## 4.2 Extended preposition system
| Prep. | Semantic space                                                                                                                                                     | IPA   |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----- |
| da    | recipient or destination; ultimately, it is the **purpose** and **target** of any act                                                                              | da    |
| dan   | **source** or **origin**; the giver as opposed to recipient or the starting point as opposed to destination                                                        | dan   |
| ye    | anything done for one's **gain** or **benefit** (e.g., running _for_ your body's health)                                                                           | je    |
| yen   | anything done to one's detriment (e.g., stealing food _for_ your coworker's detriment)                                                                             | jɛn   |
| ji    | any state of **being**; being **at** or **inside** a coordinate                                                                                                    | d͡ʑi  |
| jin   | being **out of** or **away** from any state or coordinate; exclusion from a state or coordinate                                                                    | d͡ʑɪn |
| po    | positioned **beside** or **alongside** a thing; being **in favour** or **supporting**                                                                              | po    |
| bo    | positioned **opposite** or **facing** something; resting **against** or using a thing as **support**; being **against** or acting as **substitute** (_instead of_) | bo    |
| ita   | a **piece** or **some** of a thing; _aboutness_ or an _incomplete action_                                                                                          | ita   |
| itan  | the **entirety** of a thing; _completion_ of an action; _total embodiment_ of a thing                                                                              | itan  |
| un    | **accompaniment**, **using** a tool; **emotional companionship** or an _internal state that you carry_                                                             | (ʔ)un |
| nin   | **lacking** or **being without** (a thing)                                                                                                                         | nɪn   |

## 4.3 Uses
* They're essentially used in the same way as described in:
	* **Ashi Tawa, Grammar:17-18**
* You always use the *core* preposition particles and **ONLY IF** nuance is needed, you use the other available ones
* More often than not, particles like *ye* or *yen* are not needed
* The only exception to this is *ita* and maybe some of the locative class
