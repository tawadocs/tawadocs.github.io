## 10.1 Usage
| Particle | IPA   | Gloss |
| -------- | ----- | ----- |
| ta       | `ta`  | VOC   |
| iku      | `iku` | PERS  |
* *ta* is a vocative
* It's like *oi*, *hey*, or *ey* in English
	* e.g., *ta! fuen ni fuyu no?* -> "oy! what are you doing?"
* *iku* is similar to a vocative, but simply marks a referent as a human or person
* *iku* always attaches as a complement to the complex mentioning the person
* Examples:
	* *ta Émíli'iku! zua au kamtteil hya?* -> `VOC emily-PERS 2SG-AGT HAVE.V.PLAT eat.PROD-DEF Q`
		* "Hey, Emily! Do you have the food?"
	* *sua kikita Lusi'ikuǫ* -> `1SG-AGT SUPR.like lucy-PERS-ACC`
		* "I love Lucy"
