## 18.1 Extended case usage
| Case   | Semantic space                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| -lla   | recipient or destination; ultimately, it is the **purpose** and **target** of any act                                                                              |
| -llanh | **source** or **origin**; the giver as opposed to recipient or the starting point as opposed to destination                                                        |
| -ye(t) | anything done for one's **gain** or **benefit** (e.g., running *for* your body's health)                                                                           |
| -yenh  | anything done to one's detriment (e.g., stealing food *for* your coworker's detriment)                                                                             |
| -õ    | the direct object                                                                                                                                                  |
| -ji    | any state of **being**; being **at** or **inside** a coordinate                                                                                                    |
| -jinh  | being **out of** or **away** from any state or coordinate; exclusion from a state or coordinate                                                                    |
| -po    | positioned **beside** or **alongside** a thing; being **in favour** or **supporting**                                                                              |
| -bo    | positioned **opposite** or **facing** something; resting **against** or using a thing as **support**; being **against** or acting as **substitute** (*instead of*) |
| -ita   | a **piece** or **some** of a thing; *aboutness* or an *incomplete action*                                                                                          |
| -itanh | the **entirety** of a thing; *completion* of an action; *total embodiment* of a thing                                                                              |
| -un    | **accompaniment**, **using** a tool; **emotional companionship** or an *internal state that you carry*                                                             |
| -unh   | **lacking** or **being without** (a thing)                                                                                                                         |
| -nin   | movement **through** any space or medium; **mediation** (*via*) or **causality** (*because of*); temporospatial movement                                           |
| -ninh  | **missing** or **avoiding** a point; **exclusion** or *except for*                                                                                                 |
* most of the cases or usages for cases listed in **Grammar:17** do not fully cover each domain of the case
* this table will explain the semantic range of each case

### 18.1.1 -nin and -un
* difference between **-nin** and **-un**:
	* *un* refers to a direct extension or connection
		* *ke kamurra teteun* -> you're holding the tool (hands), it is a part of your body
		* the tool (your hands) is your partner in eating and assists you
	* *nin* refers to a conduit or middleman through which you actualise your intentions
		* *ke kamurra tetenin* -> you're using the hands to achieve the goal of eating
		* the tool (your hands) is just an instrument that you're using to eat
	* the latter sounds more detached than the former and might even hint at ulterior motives ('using the hand to complete the goal which is to allow food to enter the mouth')
	* so unless there is some ulterior motive or that detachedness is intentional, just use *un* for all 'via' tasks
	* however, don't use *un* in all situations; for example, when you ask a man to help you talk to a friend, you aren't equals and you two probably don't have the same goal - so use *nin*
	* but if you two are talking to the friend together or he is assisting you talk to the friend, then you could make a good argument for using *un*
* *-nin* has additional meanings that *-un* doesn't, so in some contexts, it might be essential, such as:
	* *haha konin mo mo maku konqnin* -> `laugh day-PER then then cry day-INV-PER`
		* "Laugh (in glee) through the day, but weep through the night"
* note that *-nin* is always more visceral or strong, so in powerful metaphors like "having your heart tears mine apart with happiness I can't handle," using *-nin* is the correct choice
	* although *-nin* sounds cold and detached, here it adds to the gory metaphor, making the sentence elevated and beautiful

### 18.1.2 Benefactives and malefactives
* they can be used in this way too:
	* *irimethu twanayet* -> `work-PFV.V.PLAT device-BEN`
		* "(I) worked for the device"
		* use this if you want to say that you fixed the device
		* it's especially useful if the device is a special or intimate project for the speaker
	* *irimethu twanayenh* -> `work-PFV.V.PLAT device-MAL`
		* "(I) worked to the device's detriment"
		* this implies that you dislike the device or that you're work is actively hostile to the device
		* it can also imply sabotage

| **oljinh** | distance is higher than a comfortable or manageable threshold; beyond an expected or needed position; too removed from involvement or connection (he went too far in isolating himself); threshold violation (opposite end of a safety or acceptability boundary (e.g., experiment gone too far)); detachment, isolation (he is too distant)                              |
* the locative class of particles (ji, jinh, jiji, jinyi, jijinyi, etc.) and their semantic space
* yes, they can take on any word form (jiru, oljinhru, oljiru, iejinhtei, jistanych)
* and no, it is not as scary as it looks
* here are some sentence examples:
	* *bakalojinh* -> `circle-INDEF.PL.LOC-G.INV`
		* ~"(I am) out of balls"
	* *nuksanoljinh* -> `heart.3SG.F.POSS-EXCESS-LOC-G.INV`
		* ~"(He is) too distant from her heart"
	* *ei ni ieji à!* -> `ANAPH TOP AUG-LOC INTERJ`
		* ~"(Phew), that was close!"
	* *haekurra jistateijinyi fuka* -> `walk.V.PLAT-NEG LOC-DIM.SUP-PROD-LOC-AUG.SUP`
		* ~"Please do not walk on the floor"

## 18.2 Modern semantic spaces
| Case   | Semantic domain                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| -lla   | go to X (drive **to** London); progress towards X (from 5 PM **to** 9 PM; 6.7 metres **to** the border); do X to Y (made love **to** her; gave the book **to** Sarah); connect or join (fasten the USB **to** the port); facing (**to** the wall); perspective (**to** me, this is nice; **to** up here, the cars seem broken)                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -llanh | source (**from** a friend); origin (**from** 5 PM to 9 PM; **from** bad to worse); disconnect or unjoin ((*un*)fasten the USB **from** the port); heritage (Maki is **from** Brazil); beginning (open **from** 3 AM); indefinite start (**from** 3 PM, I won't be nice); X from (allergic **from**; banned or barred **from**; protect **from**; disallowed **from**; released **from**; made **from** / transformed **from**); distance (away **from** the stove); causal (shiver **from**; run **from**) |
| -ye(t) | do to help X; reason for X (go **for** a spin); usage of X (this house is **for** living in); motive of X (why something happened - he is famous **for** his singing); give X to get Y (pay $10 **for** the shirt); fixedness (key **for** the door; solution **for** the issue); opinion (this climate isn't right **for** me; I speak **for** the entire team); for (feel sad **for** you; wait **for** me; responsible **for**) / X **for** Y                                                           |
| -yenh  | do to X's detriment (eat **for hurting** him); reason not for X (go **not for** a spin);                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -ji    | (final) destination for verbs of motion (go **in/to** school); location of existence (pudding **in** the fridge); time (**at** 3 o'clock); recipient or target (give **to** a friend)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -un    | where an activity happens (studying **at** her home); the instrument used (going **by** train); composition (made **by/of** steel)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -nin   | through (**through** X; **via** X; **through** the night; **through** the house; **through** the crosswalk)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -ninh  | around, bypass, skirt (**around** the house); stopped by, against, into (without passing through); moving into and stopping; over, across (without depth), upon (fly **over** the house)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -po    | stance (**for** him); along (**along(side)** the house; run **along/beside** the house)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -bo    | against stance (**against** him); against (**against/opposite (to)** the house); terminative (**up until** the house)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
* These are the extra or additional meanings cases carry
* The semantic spaces of cases are huge
* Do note that a lot of cases are omitted or not distinguished in Modern Tawa

## 18.3 Case system in the Modern Tawish Register
| Case      | Modern / simplified domain                                                                                                             |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **õ**    | direct object; traversal or route of motion; point of detachment                                                                       |
| **ji**    | indirect object; location of existence; destination or final arrival point; time; goal of a movement; passive or causative             |
| **un**    | instrument or means; location of action; material or composition; cause or reason; limit                                               |
| **lla**   | heading (towards); progression                                                                                                         |
| **llanh** | temporospatial starting point or origin; source of material; source of information / giving                                            |
| **mo**    | comitative; additive; quotation or boundary of thought or sound;  comparison; temporal (and spatial) sequentiality; contrasting linker |

### 18.3.1 Explanation and definition
* Multiple cases have lost distinction in the Modern Tawish spoken register:
	* **õ**
		* direct object
			* read the *book*
		* traversal or route of *motion*
			* flew (through) the *sky*
			* walked (along) the *street*
		* point of detachment
			* graduate (from) *university*
			* step (off) the *bus*
	* **ji**
		* indirect object
			* give a gift **to** a friend
		* location of existence
			* keys are **on** the table
		* destination or final arrival point
			* we are **at** the station
			* we drove **to** London
		* time
			* writing this sentence **at** 2:42 AM
		* goal of a movement
			* went **out to** shop
		* passive or causative
			* the boss made the employee write the report
				* *komunil ni komuninqjia paya monu tetõka*
				* "the boss **made** the employee write the report"
	* **un**
		* instrument or means
			* wrote the letter **in** pen
			* ate **with** a spoon
		* location of action
			* danced **at** the club
		* material or composition
			* made **of / by / with** wood
		* cause or reason
			* the game was cancelled **due to** rain
		* limit (time/quantity/scope)
			* this shirt is cheap **for** its high quality
			* finished it **in** three days
	* **lla**
		* heading (towards)
			* heading **towards** the mountains
				* p.s., here, the focus is on 'active direction,' not the destination, unlike *ji*
		* progression
			* going **to / progressing** towards London
	* **llanh**
		* spatial starting point
			* I came here **from** Canada
		* temporal origin
			* I started **from** almost 11 to 12 o'clock
		* source of material
			* wine **from** grapes
				* p.s., here, it is about transformation or change itself; in *un*, the original material was wood, but with *llanh*, the original material has changed (grapes to wine, seed to plant, etc.)
		* source of information / giving
			* heard about it **from** her
	* **mo**
		* comitative
			* I never argue **with** my sister
		* as an additive
			* apples **and** oranges
		* quotation or boundary of a thought or sound
			* the bell rang **with** a ding
			* he said **that** he would come tonight
		* comparison
			* bigger **than** him
		* temporal sequence or sequentiality
			* ate **then** went to talk to him
		* contrasting linker
			* **but** I'm eating
			* you can do it **but** only if you pay first

### 18.3.2 Usage
* In poetry, formal works, or technical descriptions, the official Tawish case system is utilised
* In primarily *spoken* Tawa, the case system used in the **Modern Tawish spoken register** will be used
* The simple case system allows for easy conversation without needing many particles
* In conversation, the formal case system can be used, of course, but usually it shouldn't be necessary
