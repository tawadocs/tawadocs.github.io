## 17.2 Datives, allatives, and benefactives
* Examples:
	* *a ke hatero asalla* -> `AGT IMP run house-ALL`
		* "Run to the house"
	* *a ke senodzu emonol kariĉasunlla* -> `AGT IMP send.V.PLAT picture-DEF MAKE-F.PLAT-1SG.POSS-DAT`
		* "Send the picture to my mother"
	* *a kamu suyét samil sai* -> `AGT eat.V.PLAT 1SG-BEN fish-DEF AFFEC.REQ`
		* "Please eat the fish for me"
		* lit. "Please eat the fish for my benefit"

## 17.3 Ablatives, and malefactives
* Examples:
	* *a ke hateru asallanh* -> `AGT IMP run.V.PLAT house-ABL`
		* "Run (away) from the house"
	* *a ke senodzu emonol kariĉasunllanh* -> `AGT IMP send.V.PLAT picture-DEF mother-1SG.POSS-ABL`
		* "Send the picture away from my mother"
		* ~"Take away the picture from my mother"
	* *a kamu suyenh sai* -> `AGT eat.V.PLAT 1SG-MAL AFFEC.REQ`
		* ~"Please eat the fish to hurt me"
		* lit. "Please eat the fish for my detriment"

## 17.4 Accusatives
* Used for **non-o-final roots**
	* *sua kita zuǫ* -> `1SG-AGT like 2SG-ACC`
		* "I like you"
* **o-final roots** drop their **-o** and change to **-u**
	* *sua senodzu zulla emonol hya?* -> `1SG-AGT send.V.PLAT 2SG-DAT picture-DEF Q`
		* "Should I send the picture to you?"

## 17.5 Locatives
### 17.5.1 Simple location
* Examples:
	* *su ni asaji* -> `1SG TOP house-LOC`
		* lit. "(As for me), I am in house"
		* ~"I am at the house"
	* *su ni asajinh* -> `1SG TOP house-OUT`
		* "I am out of the house"

### 17.5.2 Approximative location
* You may alter the degree of location with *ie*, *ieź*, or *öl*:
	* *ieji* -> near; *iejinh* -> not near / away
	* *jiji* -> right over; *jijinh* -> far away
	* *ólji* -> too close; *óljinh* -> too far
* With *nyi* and *sta*:
	* *jinyi* -> above
	* *jista* -> below
* Examples:
	* *ribul ni asalénieji* -> `book-DEF TOP house-NEAR`
		* "The book is near a house"
	* *ribul ni asaléniejinh* -> `book-DEF TOP house-AWAY`
		* ~"The book is not near a house"
		* ~"The book is away from a house"
	* *kanojnyasun ni suóljinh* -> `partner.F.ROM-1SG.POSS TOP 1SG-EXCESS-FAR`
		* lit. "As for my girlfriend, she is too distant from me"
		* ~"My girlfriend is way too distant from me"
* These particles can also be used in comparatives:
	* *noyukssan ni soieji mo su* -> `heart.3SG.F.POSS TOP 3SG.M-NEAR than 1SG`
		* lit. "As for her heart, he is closer (to it) than me"
		* ~"He is nearer to her heart than me"

### 17.5.3 Verbal locatives
* These require the object to be accusative or for the verb to be in its accusative form
* Examples:
	* *zua hateru asalji hya?* -> `2SG-AGT run.V.PLAT house-DEF-LOC Q`
		* "Will you run into the house?"
	* *a ke hateru asaljinh* -> `AGT IMP run.V.PLAT house-DEF-ELA`
		* "Run out of the house"

## 17.6 Supportives and counter-supportives
### 17.6.1 Stance
* Examples:
	* *sa ni sopo* -> `3SG.F TOP 3SG.M-FOR`
		* ~"She is for him"
		* ~"She is for his favour"
		* ~"She is in favour of him"
	* *sa ni sobo* -> `3SG.F TOP 3SG.M-CNTR`
		* ~"She is against him"
* These can be used in spatial contexts too:
	* *su ni asapo* -> `1SG TOP house-BES`
		* ~"I am alongside the house"
	* *su ni asabo* -> `1SG TOP house-OPP`
		* ~"I am against the house"
		* ~"I am opposite to the house"
* If disambiguation is needed, append *-ji* accordingly:
	* *su ni asapjji*
	* *su ni asabjji*

### 17.6.3 Terminatives and laterals
* Examples:
	* *sua hateru asabjji* -> `1SG-AGT run.V.PLAT house-TERM`
		* ~"I run up until the house"
		* ~"I run until the house"
	* *sua hateru asapjji* -> `1SG-AGT run.V.PLAT house-LAT`
		* ~"I run along the house"
		* ~"I run beside the house"

## 17.7 Comitative and instrumental
* Examples:
	* *sua hateru foŝssunún nihi iią* -> `1SG-AGT run.V.PLAT friend.1SG.POSS-COM TOP-BACK good-MOD`
		* "Me running with my friend is good"
	* *zua kamu samiǫ naún hya?* -> `2SG-AGT eat.V.PLAT fish-ACC 1PL-COM Q`
		* "Will you eat fish with us?"
	* *a kenh kamu samilǫ tetezunún!* -> `AGT PROHIB eat.V.PLAT fish-DEF-ACC hand-2SG.POSS-INSTR`
		* "Don't eat the fish with your hands!"

### 17.7.1 Abessive
* The abessive case comes from the comitative case's inversed form *únh* (from *ún* + *-nh*)
* Examples:
	* *zu ni honkitaúnhą* -> `2SG TOP person-like-ABESS-MOD`
		* lit. "As for you, you are incapable of liking"
		* ~"You are a cold person"
		* can mean different things depending on context
	* *na ni maniúnh* -> `1PL TOP money-ABESS`
		* lit. "As for us, we are without money"
		* ~"We have no money"

## 17.8 Perlatives
* **NOTE:** The inversed form of *nín* is *nính*

### 17.8.1 Simple perlative
* Examples:
	* *rumala hateru biraknnín* -> `light-DEF-AGT run.V.PLAT curtain.PER`
		* lit. "Light runs through the curtain"
		* ~"Light shined through the curtains"
	* *sua hateru asanín* -> `1SG-AGT run.V.PLAT house-PER`
		* "I run through the house"
* In its approximative form *ienín*, it functions similarly to the English "past / by":
	* *sua hateru asaienín* -> `1SG-AGT run.V.PLAT house-AUG-PER`
		* "I run past the house"
		* "I run by the house"

### 17.8.2 Avoidative
* Examples:
	* *sua hatero asanính* -> `1SG-AGT run.V.PLAT house-AVOID`
		* "I run, avoiding the house"
	* *ruma′a hateru biraknnính* -> `light-AGT run.V.PLAT curtain.AVOID`
		* "Light shines, avoiding the curtains"
		* more idiomatically: "Light shines everywhere except the curtains"

### 17.8.3 Mediative
* Examples:
	* *a ke fofu su sonín!* -> `AGT IMP speak.V.PLAT 1SG 3SG.M-MED`
		* "Speak to me via him!"

### 17.8.4 Causal or reason
* Examples:
	* *so′o korrunai sunín* -> `3SG.M-SUFF kill.V.PLAT-IV 1SG-CAUSAL`
		* "He is dead because of me"
	* *su ni kanataĉ sonín* -> `1SG TOP sad-N.PLAT 3SG.M-CAUSAL`
		* "I am sad because of him"

## 17.9 Partitive
### 17.9.1 Partial or indefinite quantities or verbs
* Examples (quantities):
	* *sua kamrreu chiitaryą* -> `1SG-AGT eat.IPFV.V.PLAT water-PRTV-this-MOD`
		* "I will drink some of this water"
	* *honita′a kita suǫ* -> `person-PRTV-AGT like 1SG-ACC`
		* "Some (of the) people like me"
* Examples (verbs):
	* *sua senojitaru emonol* -> `1SG-AGT send.PRTV-V picture-DEF`
		* "I am not done sending the picture"
		* "I have not yet finished sending the picture"
		* "The picture has not yet been sent"

### 17.9.3 Whole quantities or verbs
* Examples (quantities):
	* *sua kamtthu samiitanhíl* -> `1SG-AGT eat.PFV.V.PLAT fish-WHOL-DEF`
		* "I ate the whole fish"
* Examples (verbs):
	* *sua kamuitanh samil* -> `1SG-AGT eat.V.PLAT-WHOL fish-DEF`
		* "I have completed eating the fish"
		* "I have eaten the fish"

### 17.9.4 Aboutness
* Use *ita* for this
* Examples:
	* *sua kitanyu saita iinyil hyo?* -> `1SG-AGT like-V.ROM 3SG.F-PRTV good-AUG.SUP-DEF OPEN.Q`
		* "What do you love the most about her?"
			* here, *saita* is used because the question is specific or evaluative and asking about an aspect of something
* It can also mean 'to circumambulate' or go around in some contexts:
	* *sua haekuthu asalita* -> `1SG-AGT walk.V.PLAT-PFV.V.PLAT house-DEF-PRTV`
		* "I walked around a house"
		* ~"I walked about a house"
		* "I learnt about a house"
* You can disambiguate by using *o* (SUFF, sufferer particle) instead of *a* to specify learning:
	* *suo haekuthu asalita* (I learnt about a house)
