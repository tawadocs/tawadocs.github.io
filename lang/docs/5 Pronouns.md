## 5.2 Genitives
* Genitives are simply marked by appending *-n* [n̻] to the regular pronominal:
	* *kamzun* `food.2SG.POSS` -> "Your food"
	* *kamsun* `food.1SG.POSS` -> "My food"
	* *asasan* `house-3SG.F.POSS` -> "Her house"

## 5.3 Different forms
* Pronominal possession markers take a *t/d* form if the word ends in the same sequence:
	* *Dasun* + *-sun* -> *dasuntsun*
	* *Dazun* + *-zun* -> *dasundzun*
* Pronunciation is self-explanatory

## 5.4 Dropping
* Pronominals can be dropped
* Examples:
	* *A kamu ya?* -> `AGT eat.V.PLAT Q`
		* "(Are you going to) eat?"

## 5.6 Anaphoric pronoun
* *ei* is an anaphoric pronoun
* It simply refers to a situation, event, or proposition
* Examples:
	* *A kamu samil hya?* -> `AGT eat.V.PLAT fish-DEF Q`
		* "(Are you going to) eat the fish?"
	* *Sua ei* -> `1SG-AGT ANAPH`
		* "I will eat it"
* If omitting the whole predicate causes too much ambiguity, then you may repeat it:
	* *A kamu sami ya?*
	* *Sua kamureu ei*
		* note: *kamureu* `eat.V.PLAT-IPFV.V.PLAT` is *kamo* + *(r)u* + *reo* + *(r)u*

## 5.7 Dummy pronoun
* *wu* can function as an expletive, like the English 'it'
* However, it must never be used this way unless absolutely necessary
* Other methods of constructing a sentence without using *wu* as an expletive are most preferred
