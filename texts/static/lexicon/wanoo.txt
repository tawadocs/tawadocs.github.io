The quintessential 'time' word. Wanoo, a garibankana, also doesn't require 'zhi' by default. So, saying 'lili wano ji' (~in a little time) is fine, whereas saying 'zhi lili wano' (~in a little time) would be redundant, even though using 'zhi' here is actually technically more grammatically correct - but wanoo is an exception to the general rule.

If 'relationships' regarding time have to be defined (before, after), then temporal particles would likely be utilised.
