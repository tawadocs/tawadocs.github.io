The verbalised/nominalised middle voice of shire (know). Please read 'Tawish Standard, Ashi Tawa (0:0.26)' to understand why 'shirenai' is used.

Shiren is the verbalised form of shire. It means 'knowing.' Shirennai is just the proper middle voice.

Shirennai is often used when there is no agent and when there is no 'active doing of knowing' (0:0.26 should explain this well, as stated above).
