Nemuari refers to 'uncertainty' or a 'wild decision.' It combines nemu (decide, choose) with the ari radical (upside-down box).

The ari radical symbolises the wilderness, which is a place fraught with indeterminacy, uncertainty.

So, nemuari is like saying 'choosing in the wild' (i.e., choosing without knowing, an equivalent of the English 'shot in the dark'). This is also why 'nemuari' is the specific word used for the Uncertainty Principle (Nemuari Shitei).
