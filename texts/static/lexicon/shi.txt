Shi is a conditional particle; however, depending on pronunciation, it can also mean principle or primordial unit.

Shi [ɕɪ̥~ɕɪ] is the pronunciation used for 'fundamental' and shi [ɕi̥~ɕi] is used for the conditional. Here's a sentence with the conditional: 'Reo ea shi so a kiwinq fofensonoe!' ((I/we) will go if he shuts up).

In the Ashi Tawa standard, the difference is usually orthographically marked (shi (conditional) vs. shí (unit)), but Koyeah Tawa has dropped this distinction.
