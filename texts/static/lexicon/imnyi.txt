The augmentative superlative of the similative 'imo.' It is essentially an equative, like 'as' in English.

'Zu imnyi atora' would mean 'you are an apple' or 'you as an apple.' You'd typically see something like that in: 'shi to zu imnyi atora...' (if you were an apple...) or 'imnyi atora, ppai kikita' (as an apple, (I) would love myself).

It does not carry an innate or inherent irreal connotation, since you also see it in constructions like 'fofurra su imnyi "Mikomiko," fuka' (please, don't call me (as) 'Mikomiko').
