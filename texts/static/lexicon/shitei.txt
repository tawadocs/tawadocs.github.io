This is the product of either the conditionality particle shi [ɕi̥~ɕi] or the primordial unit root [ɕɪ̥~ɕɪ].

As the product of the primordial unit root, it literally means 'principle,' 'unit,' or 'fundamental.'

As a product of the conditionality particle, it is derived similarly to jitei (place, position, from the product of the bare locative). So, if jitei, as a product of the bare locative refers to place or position, then shitei, as a product of the bare conditional, could refer to the condition, situation, premise, or (set of) circumstances.
