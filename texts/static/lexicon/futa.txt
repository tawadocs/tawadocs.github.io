Futa is borrowed from the Japanese word ふた, which refers to a simple lid. In Tawa, futa refers to caps, lids, or any covering that seals, secures, or protects the contents of a container.

This 'container' can refer to the human head, a house's top, or a simple bottle. In these contexts, futa, respectively, would refer to a hat or cap, a roof, and a bottlecap or lid.

Furthermore, futa's domain extends to bottlenecks or limitations. For example, saying 'shiretei no nampa me shirenaitas ue ni myo ue' (the amount of information one can learn is a lot).
