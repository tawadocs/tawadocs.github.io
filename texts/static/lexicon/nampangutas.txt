The potential form of the lexical source of nampa (amount, number). It refers to the potential to be measured.

Nampangutas is formed from nampangu and tas. Nampangu breaks down into nampa and ngu. Nampa refers to amount or number, and ngu refers to the semantic lexical source or category. Together, nampangu refers to measure or quantity, as numbers and amounts are a type of measure and quantity. Adding -tas just marks potential, similarly to -able in English.

Together: nampangutas, therefore, refers to measurability.
