Gayanu is the modifier form of 'gaya,' which refers to physicality, bodies, or matter and materiality itself. For example, gayatachi would refer to materialistic ideologies (such as materialism itself) and gayatei would just refer to matter. Gayasumata, though, would refer to physicality as a concept in and of itself.

In the modifier form, gaya can be a qualifier. 'gayanu kitei' (physical thing) would refer to a literal, material, or physical 'thing.'
