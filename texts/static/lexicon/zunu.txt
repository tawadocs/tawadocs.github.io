The adjective form of 'zu,' which is most commonly used as the second person pronoun 'you.' As a modifier, 'zu' (as 'zunu') refers to duality or secondariness.

It often never refers to 'you-ness,' because what even is that? You could say 'zunu hon' (a you-person / a second person), but this is not a common construction. Instead, we utilise similatives (e.g., 'ryo hon imo zu' - this person is like you) or other particles.
