Kitei refers to a 'thing.' Any 'thing' that is a 'thing' is a kitei. This world, God, universe, life, death, you, me, your cat, my parrot, and my friend are all kitei.

What isn't a kitei is much more interesting though: a square-circle is not a thing and neither is a world without God.

This means that: ontological inconsistencies and modal impossibilities are non-things (kiteinq). Necessary truths (something exists, 2+2=4) are things (kitei), but something that violates a necessary truth (nothing exists, 2+2=5) is a non-thing (kiteinq).
