## 6.1 Particles
| Particle | IPA   | Gloss   |
| -------- | ----- | ------- |
| nyi      | `nʲi` | AUG.SUP |
| sta      | `sta` | DIM.SUP |
| ie       | `ie`  | AUG     |
| ól      | `ɔl`  | EXCESS  |

## 6.2 Intensification and diminution
* Order often does not matter
* Examples:
	* *sa ni kanojnyasunsta* -> `3SG.F TOP partner.F.ROM-1SG.POSS-DIM.SUP`
		* "She is someone I just started dating"
	* *sa ni kanojnyi* -> `3SG.F TOP partner.AUG.SUP`
		* "She is my spouse"
	* *raŝimasnyi* -> `teach-AG-AUG.SUP`
		* "Full professor, guru"
	* *raŝimassta* -> `teach-AG-DIM.SUP`
		* "Student, learner"

## 6.3 Augmentatives, excessives, and comparatives
* By default, *ie* and *ól* function as augmentatives and excessives
* However, with context, they may be used as comparatives
* Examples (non-comparative):
	* *samiryą ni ieii!* -> `fish-this-MOD TOP AUG-good`
		* "This fish is pretty good!"
	* *samiryą ni ii′ii!* -> `fish-this-MOD TOP SUPR-good`
		* "This fish is really good!" / "This fish is the best!"
	* *samiryą ni nanaŝita!* -> `fish-this-MOD TOP SUPR-warm`
		* "This fish is extremely warm!"
		* ~"This fish is too hot!"
	* *samiryą ni ólnaŝita* -> `fish-this-MOD TOP EXCESS-warm`
		* "This fish is overwhelmingly scorching, boiling hot"
* Examples (comparative):
	* *asasun ni iepoki mo asazun* -> `house-1SG.POSS TOP AUG-big than house-2SG.POSS`
		* "My house is bigger than yours"
	* *mirol ni ólpoki mo ovsàki* -> `mirror-DEF TOP EXCESS-big than door`
		* "The mirror is too big for the door"
