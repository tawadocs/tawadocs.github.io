## 3.1 Syllable structure and stress
* The syllable structure is simply (C)V(C)
* Stress always falls on the first syllable, so always ['kanata], never [ka'nata]
* Roots must follow (C)V(N)

## 3.2 Vowel changes
* The vowel *o* can drop and the first consonant or vowel of the suffix is simply attached
* However, if the sound preceding the *o* is *r*, then *o* does not drop, but if the affix starts with an *-r* then the *o* is dropped in favour of *rr* [ɾ]
* The *o* is not dropped if the verb accepts an affix which has a verbal form
* The *o* is dropped and replaced with a verb marker if it accepts an object and if the affix does not have a verbal form
* If the consonant cluster is something like *C* + a liquid, then the *o* is preserved (like *emono* + *l* yields *emonol*)
* If either plosives or dentialveolars have to cluster with one of their own family, like *senodo* + *to* as *senodto*, then the *o* is also preserved
* If liquids have to cluster with their own kind, like *karo* + *l* as *karol*, then the *o* is preserved

## 3.3 Banned syllables
* *t* cannot appear in the forms *ti* *tį* or *tí*; instead, *ĉi ĉį* or *ĉí* is used or *thi thí* or *thį* is used
* *d* cannot also appear in those forms; instead, *ji jį* or *jí* is used
* *s* nor *z* cannot appear before *i į* or *í*; instead, *ŝi ŝį ŝí* and *ẑi ẑį ẑí* are used

## 3.4 Morphophonemics
* The particle *a* does not fuse in any way
* Like, for example, *so* + *-a* would be *soa* and the *o* wouldn't be dropped
* If *a* has to appear before another *a*, like in *sa*, then a glottal stop is introduced in-between; therefore, *sa* + *-a* becomes *sa′a*
* If a nasalised sound, such as *ą*, appears as an affix after a root ending in the same sound - only not nasalised - than simply that sound is nasalised:
	* *asa* + *-ą* becomes *asą* simply
* Grammatical particles must try to maintain as much of their authentic form as possible, so that is why:
	* *to* + *icu* is *thiicu*, not *ĉiicu*
* Particles like reo do not omit the *o* when fusing:
	* *reo* + *ppu* -> *reoppu* (**never** reppu/repppu!)

## 3.5 Nasal palatalisation
* The alveolopalatal nasal *nʲ* becomes a palatal nasal *ɲ* in consonant clusters like *nyĉ* [ɲtɕ]

## 3.6 Bilabials and labiodentals
* If *v* or *v'* ever cluster with a bilabial, they are realised as [w] and [w̃]
* So *fva* or *bv'o* becomes [ɸwa] and [bw̃õ]~[bw̃o] respectively
