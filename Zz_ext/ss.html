akesi linja

kowaisoho (kowo+aiso+ho) is the word used for "snake." It can refer to actual snakes (kikitanq kowaisohlóõ (I hate snakes)) or 'snakes' in the figurative sense, like how we use rat in English slang - 'kowaisohychsubekã à' (all snakes!  - referring to people as sly, backstabbers, or betrayers). You can use it as a verb, in which it means slither, like 'soa kowaisofu' (he slithered (like a snake)). The verbal meaning can also be figurative, as in betray or backstab. kowaisoho can also be used as a vulgar or crude word for a penis. kowaisohot is the negative form (backstab, betray), and kowaisohóy would be the more 'positive' form (sneaky, sly). Though calling your friend "kowaisoho" would be quite rude, so the positive form is mandatory, plus using another word is highly encouraged - 'lilikowaisohóy' or 'sawakowaisohóy' (little sneaky (person) or sweet and cunning respectively).
